# Task 4: Research Assistance

## Objective
To create an AI prompt that efficiently gathers, synthesizes, and evaluates information on complex workplace topics to support fast, data-driven decision-making.

---

## Master Prompt Template

**Role:** You are a senior research analyst and subject matter expert.  
**Task:** Conduct a concise research analysis on the topic provided below.

**Guidelines & Output Format:**
- **Executive Summary:** High-level key findings in 2–3 sentences.
- **Core Insights / Comparison:** Main points structured using bullet points or a comparative table.
- **Pros & Cons / Key Considerations:** Balanced view highlighting advantages and potential risks.
- **Actionable Recommendation:** Clear next steps or best practices for implementation.
- **Constraints:** Use factual, clear language and avoid overly technical jargon unless defined.

**Input Variables:**
- **Research Topic:** [e.g., Comparing Notion AI vs. ChatGPT Enterprise for team collaboration]
- **Target Audience:** [e.g., Department Management / Non-technical Team]
- **Key Questions to Answer:** [e.g., Cost, ease of adoption, data security]

---

## Example Usage & Output

### Input Provided to AI:
- **Research Topic:** Best practices for introducing AI tools to a workforce while ensuring data security.
- **Target Audience:** Department Heads and HR Managers.
- **Key Questions to Answer:** How to protect sensitive company data, key policies needed, and how to encourage team adoption.

### AI Generated Output:

### Executive Summary
Successfully adopting AI in the workplace requires balancing innovation with strict data security measures. Organizations must implement clear usage policies, train staff on privacy risks, and select enterprise-grade AI tools that do not train on proprietary data.

---

### Key Pillars of Safe AI Adoption

1. **Data Protection & Privacy**
   - Ensure AI platforms follow compliance standards and do not store sensitive prompt data.
   - Prohibit employee input of personally identifiable information (PII), confidential client data, or internal financial records.

2. **Policy & Governance**
   - Establish an explicit Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) for generative AI tools.
   - Define clear human-in-the-loop review requirements before publishing AI-generated content.

3. **Change Management & Adoption**
   - Host practical, role-specific workshops to demonstrate productivity benefits.
   - Encourage prompt-sharing channels within teams to showcase successful workplace applications.

---

### Comparison: Open AI Tools vs. Enterprise AI Deployment

| Feature | Open/Free AI Tools | Enterprise AI Deployment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Privacy** | High risk (Inputs may train public models) | Low risk (Private data boundaries guaranteed) |
| **Cost** | Free or low-cost per user | Higher subscription fee |
| **Control & Oversight** | Minimal administrator oversight | Centralized admin access & security controls |

---

### Actionable Recommendation
Start by drafting a 1-page "AI Usage Guideline" for the team, then run a pilot program with standard non-sensitive tasks (e.g., meeting summaries, template drafting) before expanding to operational workflows.
