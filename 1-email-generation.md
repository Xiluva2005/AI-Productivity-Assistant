# Task 1: Email Generation

## Objective
To create a structured prompt that generates clear, professional, and context-appropriate workplace emails in seconds.

---

## Master Prompt Template

**Role:** You are an expert executive communication assistant.  
**Task:** Draft a professional email based on the details provided below.  

**Guidelines:**
- **Tone:** Professional, direct, and polite.
- **Structure:**
  - Clear, concise subject line.
  - Professional greeting.
  - Core message (1–2 short paragraphs).
  - Clear action item or call to action (if applicable).
  - Professional sign-off.
- **Constraints:** Avoid overly complex jargon and keep the message brief and easy to scan.

**Input Variables:**
- **Recipient:** [e.g., Project Lead, Team, Client]
- **Purpose of Email:** [e.g., Request project extension, follow up on pending task, announce meeting]
- **Key Details to Include:** [e.g., Reason for request, new target date, current progress]

---

## Example Usage & Output

### Input Provided to AI:
- **Recipient:** Project Supervisor (Sarah)
- **Purpose of Email:** Request a 2-day extension on the weekly progress report
- **Key Details to Include:** Unexpected system downtime on Tuesday delayed data collection; report will be ready by Thursday at 12:00 PM.

### AI Generated Output:

**Subject:** Request for Extension: Weekly Progress Report

Hi Sarah,

I hope you are having a good week.

I am writing to request a brief 2-day extension on the submission of this week’s progress report. Due to unexpected system downtime on Tuesday, our team experienced a temporary delay in collecting the necessary data. 

We are back on track and finishing up the final analysis. I will have the completed report delivered to you by **Thursday at 12:00 PM**.

Thank you for your understanding, and please let me know if you have any immediate questions.

Best regards,  
Xiluva
